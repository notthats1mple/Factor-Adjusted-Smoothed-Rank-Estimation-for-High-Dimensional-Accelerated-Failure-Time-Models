\begin{abstract}
Factor adjustment separates common and feature-specific variation in high-dimensional accelerated failure time (AFT) regression. Under right censoring, the estimated factor coordinates must be connected to the original sparse slope. We develop a smoothed rank estimator with unpenalized clinical and factor effects and an $\ell_1$ penalty on molecular coefficients. An exact reference representation preserves the ordinary-model predictor and separates design perturbation from additional-factor approximation. Under common-error, conditional-censoring, local-information, and factor-design conditions, finite-sample coefficient bounds require no moments of the log-time error. The analysis accommodates factors estimated from the same covariate sample. Column-scale control of pairwise fluctuations avoids an additional logarithmic loss. A full-coordinate stationarity criterion transfers the bounds to numerical candidates. In three fixed strong-dependence samples, factor adjustment has lower maximum active curvature and uses fewer gradient evaluations to reach common accuracy. One active-face Newton step completes each unadjusted fit. Training selection yields twelve fits at the prescribed tolerance and distinguishes sparse residual effects from dense factor-mediated prediction.

\end{abstract}
