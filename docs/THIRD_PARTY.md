# Third-party material

`manuscript/svjour3.cls` and `manuscript/svglov3.clo` were supplied with the v0.16 manuscript. Their bytes are retained and hashed in `provenance/source-v016.json`. The original package describes them as textual transports from the public `latextemplates/svjour` mirror, with comments and whitespace condensed.

The mirror now resolves to [latextemplates/svjour-enhanced](https://github.com/latextemplates/svjour-enhanced). Its [upstream README](https://github.com/latextemplates/svjour-enhanced/blob/main/README.md), checked on 2026-10-14, attributes the Springer class to Springer and restricts its use to submissions to Springer journals. This manuscript is formatted for Lifetime Data Analysis. Those third-party conditions remain separate from the authors' manuscript and project scripts; no project license overrides them.

The project-specific typography override is `manuscript/lda-review12.sty`. Template acquisition details remain in [the original provenance note](../template_provenance/README.md).

Fonts are embedded in the supplied PDF/EPS resources. Standalone font files are not distributed.
